1. Why v0.2 Felt Abrupt vs. How v0.3 Smooth Motion Works

In your uploaded v0.2 project, dispatchTouchEvent waited until dy < -dp(64) and then called setContentView() or opened a static Dialog. In v0.3, all layers (3-page homeStrip, appDrawerLayer, navDrawerLayer, and volumeOverlayCard) stay alive inside a single hardware-accelerated FrameLayout.

2. Real-Time Finger Tracking & Velocity Fling

As your finger moves in ACTION_MOVE, homeOffsetPx, appDrawerProgress, and navDrawerProgress update continuously every frame, driving setTranslationX(), setTranslationY(), setScaleX/Y(), and Android 12+ RenderEffect.createBlurEffect(). On ACTION_UP, VelocityTracker computes swipe velocity in px/sec to settle smoothly.

// Continuous 1:1 Touch Transform in MainActivity.java (v0.3)
homeStrip.setTranslationX(-homeOffsetPx);

homeStage.setScaleX(1f - overlay * 0.08f);

    homeStage.setRenderEffect(RenderEffect.createBlurEffect(r, r, Shader.TileMode.CLAMP));
}
